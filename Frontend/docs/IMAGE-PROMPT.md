# 3D 대표 에셋 생성 프롬프트

Built-in image generation 도구를 사용했다.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: mobile app onboarding and empty-state hero illustration
Primary request: a single friendly 3D scene combining a rounded map location pin with a tiny camera lens, communicating real-time place video and crowd awareness
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background for local background removal; background must be one uniform color with no gradient, texture, floor, reflections, or lighting variation
Subject: one central rounded location pin in pale sky blue, with a small dark charcoal camera lens inset and one restrained lime #C4E600 accent ring; simple silhouette, no more than two connected objects
Style/medium: soft-plastic 3D icon, matte surface, subtle semi-gloss, pastel palette, low contrast, friendly and minimal
Composition/framing: centered, front-right three-quarter view around 20 degrees, generous padding, fully visible object
Lighting/mood: soft diffused light from upper left, gentle and trustworthy
Color palette: pale blue, #3B3838 charcoal, #C4E600 lime only; maximum three colors
Materials/textures: smooth matte soft plastic, no surface texture
Constraints: no text, no letters, no logo, no watermark; crisp separated edges; no cast shadow, no contact shadow; do not use #ff00ff anywhere in the subject
Avoid: glass, metallic reflections, photorealism, extra decorations, additional objects, strong highlights, saturated multicolor palette
```

추가로 온보딩 선호 장소 선택용 소프트 플라스틱 장소 클러스터와 크레딧 적립용 라임 토큰을 built-in 이미지 생성 도구로 제작했다. 두 에셋 모두 흰색·연한 라임·연한 블루 배경, 좌측 상단 확산광, 낮은 명암 대비, 3색 이내 원칙을 적용했다.
